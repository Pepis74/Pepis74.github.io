This was a game made for the 2020 GMTK game jam in collaboration with one other programmer and an artist. In keeping with the game jam’s theme, Out of Control, in this turn-based game the player takes the role of a cat-sitter. They must rearrange furniture to protect their friend’s precious items from his unpredictable pet.

There are 5 valuables the player must stop the cat from destroying. An ash urn, a jasmine pot, a computer, the jacket in the coat hanger and an aquarium. In order to do that, the player can move a valuable during his turn. And in the subsequent turn the cat will move, going after these valuables. And so on. Aside from relocating the objects you must defend, you can also move the chairs, the nightstand or the lamp to get in the way of the cat's path or use them as bait to protect the valuables.
