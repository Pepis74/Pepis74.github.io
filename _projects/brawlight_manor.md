Brawlight Manor is a competitive couch 1v1 game set in a metaphorically and literally dark and mysterious mansion where light is your most valuable resource. You must choose the character that fits your style and battle your opponent for the control of the crystals scattered throughout the map. Whoever has the most crystals under their control by the end of the match wins. You can discover more about the game and download it by visting our [itch.io](https://pusiriot.itch.io/brawlight-manor).

This game was developed as the main coursework for the videogame development class in my Computer Graphics, Games and Virtual Reality Master's degree at Universidad Rey Juan Carlos. 6 students, including me, banded together and formed Pusi Riot. And together we made Brawlight Manor. My official role in the team was lead game designer, responsible for gameplay, character and narrative design. But, I also worked on coding and VFX together with other team members.
